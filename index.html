Scheduling System

Aeroflow scheduling system enables the Aero Dynamist principal to efficiently assign objectives, dates, and hours to multiple team members—while providing clear, real-time feedback and multi-channel notifications—then the team will experience improved clarity, reduced scheduling conflicts, and higher engagement with project objectives.

UX

Mapped the aero dynamist's weekly workflow to pinpoint where scheduling conflicts and missed hours originated, then mapped front-stage actions against back-stage validation logic to catch failure points invisible in the UI alone.

UI

Tested drag-and-drop against click-to-assign and kept both, since usability testing showed users wanted speed for bulk edits but control for single-hour changes. Iterated the color-coding system after testing revealed users confused "conflict" and "overbooked" under the same red.

R&D Workshops

Ran workshops with aero dynamists and the team principal to surface where scheduling actually broke down — not just what the system needed to do.

Problem

Assigning complex, multi-week objectives to several aero dynamists, ensuring no conflicts, and providing clear visibility of assignments and missing hours.

Success and Failure

Defined what "working" looked like upfront (zero missed assignments, no hour conflicts) so the design had a measurable target, not just a feature list.

Service Blueprint

Mapped front-stage actions (assigning hours, viewing the calendar) against back-stage logic (validation, conflict detection) to catch failure points invisible in the UI alone.

Mastercard Cash Passport

Ran a collaborative workshop to capture real CSR pain points before mapping flows across all three roles, then decomposed Sell Card into a four-step guided pattern that keeps CSRs oriented on live calls. Applied progressive disclosure to keep KYC fields minimal for most calls while meeting regulatory requirements for edge cases like minors.

Senior UX/UI Lead · Mastercard Cash Passport · End-to-end redesign of a 3-role portal (Admin, CSR, Branch) across Mobile and Web — cut the Sell Card flow to 4 guided steps and reduced CSR errors through progressive disclosure and inline validation.

empathy

CSRs need to move fast on live calls while staying accurate — every extra field, ambiguous state, or unclear validation costs them time on the phone and risks a downstream error.

ideate

Mapped detailed user flows across all three roles and permission levels before deciding on structure, then chose a stepper pattern over a single dense form.

solution

Design principles applied: progressive disclosure (reduces cognitive load without hiding regulatory requirements), recognition over recall (the stepper always shows CSRs where they are), and error prevention.

test

Iterated the flow with the team until it felt simple and intuitive on live-call conditions, prioritising speed and clarity over visual density.

AI Medical Editor, Builder, Simulator and Execution (start-up)

Directed strategic research and discovery sessions to pinpoint product vision, user pain points, and business KPIs, then designed the end-to-end AI-assisted platform for editing, building, simulating, and executing dynamic medical forms — validated with a 93% approval rate in usability testing with real users.

For this project I used Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, and Lumo for research and ideation support; integrated Figma Make for rapid discovery and ideation, enabling faster, data-driven iteration.

problem

Medical teams need a way to build and validate dynamic forms — but without an integrated system, editing, simulation, and execution live in separate tools or manual steps, increasing risk of error and slowing iteration.

define

Bhrigu needed an AI-powered platform where medical form creation, simulation, and execution live in one workflow — reducing the gap between building a form and trusting it's ready for real use.

ideate

Directed strategic research and discovery sessions to pinpoint product vision, user pain points, and business KPIs before defining structure — grounding the AI editor/builder/simulator/execution flow in real user needs rather than assumed technical capability.

test

Validated the platform with real users through usability testing, achieving a 93% approval rate — confirming the design's adoption readiness among the target user group.

Common Review System

The Fund aimed to modernise its outdated eReview system for managing reviews. Because the application was unhandy, the goal was to create a more intuitive and personalised experience to improve user comfort and trust.

Conducted granular research to understand how users navigated the legacy eReview system, uncovering frustration around lack of control, transparency, and clarity. Redesigned the system around role-specific permissions and centralized visibility — scaling the solution across every Fund product and document security classification rather than solving for a single use case.

empathy

I conducted granular research to understand how users initiated and navigated the review process, identifying pain points with the legacy system. I discovered that users were frustrated by the lack of control, transparency, and clarity in managing and commenting on reviews.

problem

The Fund relied on an outdated eReview system for managing reviews. Usability was unhandy, and the goal was to create a more intuitive and personalized experience to improve user comfort and trust — modernizing a system central to how the Fund conducts its work.

solution

Redesigned the review management system, focusing on streamlining the document handling process and improving user engagement throughout the review stages. The result: a modernised, role-specific experience covering all review stages — from interdepartmental review to management clearance, corrections, and deletions.

touch points

  • New and modernised user experience
  • Improved departmental review management and online commenting
  • Includes all review stages from interdepartmental review to management clearance, corrections and deletions
  • Enabled for all document security classifications, with permissions managed centrally within CRS

Grading App

Translated a dense, paper-based speaking mark sheet — six grading categories scored across a 0–5.0 scale, plus seating and multi-candidate tracking — into a mobile-first grading flow. Prioritised clarity and speed for examiners working in real time, with secure, booking-date-linked access ensuring only the assigned examiner can grade a given session.

hypothesis

People are not used anymore to grading students on paper-based documents.

problem

The paper-based speaking (grading) mark sheet needs to be translated into a mobile application, with which an examiner can mark their speaking tests — while ensuring secure, scheduled access tied to each candidate's booking date, since exam integrity depends on the right examiner grading the right candidate at the right time.

solution

As a product designer, I present a preliminary concept for a mobile Minimum Viable Product (MVP) aimed at transforming the grading experience for examiners. This application prioritizes clarity, transparency, usability, and fluidity in its interactions, ensuring a seamless operation from the moment examiners log in to the final submission of grades.

outcome

This proposed mobile MVP embodies the principles of clarity, transparency, usability, and fluidity in its design. By focusing on the examiner's needs and streamlining the grading process, we can enhance user experience significantly while maintaining the integrity and security of the examination process.

Invoicing App

Invoicing & Payments App · Designed the onboarding journey and mobile interaction model for a business app helping users create invoices, receive payments, and manage both in one place.

UX

Structured onboarding into three distinct stages — pre-Sage ID, post-Sage ID data capture, and first landing — to reduce friction at each handoff point and directly address the moment users ask "what next?" after setup.

UI

Applied Thumb Zone Design principles to the mobile interface, placing primary actions like invoice creation and payment confirmation within the Natural Zone at the bottom of the screen for effortless one-handed reach, while keeping the Hard Zone at the top free of interactive elements to avoid forcing users to stretch or shift grip.

hypothesis

Based on the onboarding structure, a reasonable placeholder: "If business owners can move from first app download to a completed invoice/payment setup with minimal friction before and after Sage ID creation, activation and retention will improve." Replace with your real hypothesis if you had one.

empathy

Who is the Sage user, specifically — sole traders, small business owners, freelancers? What do they need emotionally/practically in this moment (e.g., "I need to get paid without chasing clients" or "I don't have time to learn a complex tool")? This is foundational to the rest of the case study and worth writing first.

ideate

Did you consider a single-stage or shorter onboarding flow before landing on three stages? Why split pre-ID and post-ID data capture instead of combining them? This is a real design decision with a visible trade-off (thoroughness vs. friction) — worth naming even briefly.

solution

Designed the mobile experience using Thumb Zone Design principles: primary actions (like creating an invoice or confirming a payment) placed in the Natural Zone — the bottom third of the screen, reachable without adjusting grip. Secondary actions placed in the Stretch Zone, requiring slightly more reach.

Booking Appointment

Designed a dual-path booking flow for both authenticated users and guests, addressing the navigation complexity and unclear confirmation communication that drive missed vaccination appointments. Prioritized real-time, location-based availability and immediate confirmation to directly close the trust gap identified in research.

UX

Identified that current vaccination booking systems fail users through complicated navigation and unclear confirmation communication — a gap directly tied to missed appointments and no-shows. Designed a dual-path flow accommodating both authenticated users and guests, so account creation friction never becomes a barrier to a time-sensitive health booking. Prioritized real-time, location-based slot availability to reduce wasted navigation and help users find relevant appointments faster.

UI

Built clear call-to-action buttons guiding users into distinct pathways — one for authenticated users, one for guests — so the interface itself signals which route fits each visitor without added explanation. Designed immediate email confirmation and reminder touchpoints directly into the flow, closing the communication gap identified as a root cause of no-shows, and surfaced available locations dynamically based on the user's geographic area to keep the booking interface relevant rather than overwhelming.

problem

Current systems for booking vaccination appointments often present challenges in usability and accessibility. Many users are frustrated by complicated navigation, lengthy processes, and a lack of clear communication regarding appointment confirmations. This can lead to missed appointments, increased no-show rates, and lower overall participation in vaccination campaigns. Addressing these concerns is crucial for Sobeys to foster trust and engagement with the community.

define

The design challenge is to create a cohesive and user-friendly appointment booking flow that enhances the experience for both authenticated users and guests. Key user requirements include:

  • Simple navigation and clear labeling throughout the booking process
  • Real-time availability of appointment slots based on location

ideate

This is the strongest opportunity in this case study to show reasoning, because the guest-vs-authenticated split is a real design fork with trade-offs. Worth a line on: did you consider forcing account creation for everyone (simpler to build, but adds friction and likely increases drop-off for a time-sensitive health booking)?

solution

A well-designed flow caters to both authenticated users and guests, ensuring accessibility for all potential customers. By implementing this solution, Sobeys can improve the overall user experience, foster greater community engagement in vaccination efforts, and ensure a more organized and effective appointment booking process.
